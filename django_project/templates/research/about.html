Biography

Dr. Saidi Kapiga is a distinguished Professor of Epidemiology and International Health at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (LSHTM) and Scientific Director of the Mwanza Intervention Trials Unit (MITU) in Tanzania.

With over 30 years of public health research experience, Dr. Kapiga has dedicated his career to investigating the individual risk factors and underlying structural factors associated with HIV and other health problems in Africa. His work focuses particularly on the evaluation of public health interventions across diverse populations in East and Southern Africa.

Dr. Kapiga is currently involved in a range of groundbreaking studies conducted in Mwanza city, including clinical trials to assess the impact of social-worker interventions on reducing post-hospital mortality among HIV patients, observational studies to determine the incidence and determinants of hypertension among HIV-infected and uninfected adults and adolescents, and studies to develop and evaluate intervention programmes for adolescent health screening.

He serves as a Co-Principal Investigator of the MRC-funded study to investigate substance use disorders among men in urban and rural communities in South Africa, and is a member of several research and capacity development programmes in East Africa, including the Wellcome Trust funded THRiVE to Research Excellence consortium and the Lake Victoria Consortium for Health Research.

Academic Contributions

In addition to his research activities, Dr. Kapiga is actively involved in teaching students undertaking distance learning courses in epidemiology, public health, infectious diseases, and clinical trials. He currently serves as the Chair of the Board of Examiners in MSC, PG Diploma, and PG certificate in Clinical Trials at LSHTM.
